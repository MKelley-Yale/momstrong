# MOMstrong Workout App — Setup Guide

**Version 1.0 — completed 2026-07-27**

One file (`workout.html`) is the whole app. It runs offline; all data stays on your phone.

## 1. Get the file onto your iPhone

Pick one:

**Option A — Wi-Fi transfer (easiest from a PC)**
1. On iPhone, open Documents (Readdle) → tap **+** or the **...** menu → **Import from computer** (or go to the Wi-Fi Transfer feature).
2. On your PC, open the address it shows (docstransfer.com) in a browser and enter the 4-digit code.
3. Drag `workout.html` from `D:\Claude Projects\MOMstrong` into the browser window.

**Option B — Email**
1. Email `workout.html` to yourself as an attachment.
2. On iPhone, long-press the attachment → **Share** → **Documents** (or Save to Files, then copy it into Documents).

**Option C — iCloud Drive**
1. Put `workout.html` in iCloud Drive from your PC (icloud.com or iCloud for Windows).
2. In Documents, browse to iCloud Drive and **copy the file into "My Files"** (local), so it works offline.

## 2. Open it

1. In Documents, tap `workout.html`. It opens in Documents' built-in viewer and runs like an app.
2. Do one exercise and check it off, then close and reopen — confirm the checkmark is still there. That verifies data is saving.

**Make it fast to reach:** long-press the file → **Add to Favorites** (or drag it to the top level of My Files). Opening Documents → one tap = workout.

## 3. First-time setup (2 minutes)

1. **Settings tab → Band difficulty order**: arrange your 9 band colors easiest → hardest (▲/▼), remove any you don't have, tap **Save order**. This is what lets "way too easy" auto-suggest the next band up.
2. Skim the **Library tab** — hide anything you'll never do, edit names/cues to your liking.

## 4. Backups (important on iPhone)

iOS can clear app browser storage when space is low. Your history lives in that storage, **not** in the html file.

- Every week or two: **Settings tab → Export backup**. It saves a small `momstrong-backup-DATE.json` file — keep it in Documents or iCloud.
- If history ever disappears: **Settings → Restore from backup** → pick the newest json.

## 5. Updating the app later

When you get a new version of `workout.html`:

1. **Export a backup first.**
2. Replace the old file with the new one — **same filename, same folder**. Data normally carries over; if it doesn't, restore from the backup.

## Daily use cheat-sheet

- Top strip: stairs count (− / +), Walk, Yoga (+ minutes), and a Steps box (type today's total from Apple Health — see STEPS-SHORTCUT.md for a one-tap helper).
- Cards: one per muscle, order and exercise/stretch mix re-randomized daily. "Last" = what you did last time; "Try" = today's target (already bumped up/down based on how last time felt).
- Checkbox (top-right of card) → quick log, prefilled — just tap Save. Card greys out and drops to the bottom.
- Stretch cards have a timer: pick length in the dropdown, tap Start.
- ▶ how-to videos = YouTube search for that exact move.
- Calendar tab = what you did any day. Stats tab = graphs per muscle.
